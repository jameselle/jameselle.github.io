Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy covers the automatic Instagram replies run by $1M in 365 Days (Instagram @jameselle). When you comment a keyword on one of our posts, or send us that keyword in a direct message, our tool replies and sends you the link you asked for.

What we receive

From Instagram, for comments and messages sent to our account only: your Instagram username, the Instagram-scoped ID Instagram gives our account for you, the text of your comment or message, when you sent it, and whether you follow our account (checked only after you have messaged us).

What we use it for

Only to reply to your comment, send you the link you asked for, and avoid messaging you twice. We don't sell, share or rent it, use it for advertising, or combine it with other data.

Where it is kept, and for how long

On our own computer, not in a third-party service. We keep a record of which comments and messages we replied to for up to 90 days, then delete it. The messages themselves stay in your Instagram conversation with us, under Instagram's own policy.

Deleting your data

Send us a direct message on Instagram saying "delete my data", or email the1min365days@proton.me. We'll remove our records about you within 30 days and confirm when it's done. You can also remove our access from Instagram at any time: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites.
